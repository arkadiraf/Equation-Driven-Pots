5. Mesh Resolution
When enabled, Vertical and Radial Nodes above are computed automatically from how much angular/vertical detail the current base equation actually needs, instead of a fixed number.
6. Experimental Tool - Field Modifier
Field modifiers remap the sampled coordinates before evaluating the selected target, sharing the same strength and target above. The angular modifier warps theta; the optional vertical warp additionally remaps v (recomputing z or phi to match), enabling conformal warps and vertical feature migration. Leave the vertical warp blank for the original angular-only behavior.
7. Experimental Tool - Field Distortion
Distortion fields act after the base and texture fields are sampled, displacing the generated body in world XYZ space. Use wind, gravity, swirl, or pressure presets as starting points, then tune region and anchor behavior.
