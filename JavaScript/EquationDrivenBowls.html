Equation Driven Bowls

1. Bowl Form
Lamp Mode bores a central hole down through the solid base (for a cord or bulb fitting).
A separate round ring (torus) stand below the bowl, for profiles whose base isn't flat (e.g. Bottom Angle 180°). Ring Radius sets the tube's centreline radius; Ring Width sets the tube diameter. The bowl rests on top of the ring. Ring Cut slices a flat base off the bottom of the tube so it sits stably (0 = full round tube, untouched).

3. Dual Pattern Color Layer
Two masks define four surface color states: neither, A only, B only, and A+B overlap. Exported to .3mf as a four-color group.
When on, Mask A/B are also sampled on the bottom disk, cavity floor and (when Base Pattern is active, cylindrical only) the perforated base. When off, the base always uses the Neither/Base color.
